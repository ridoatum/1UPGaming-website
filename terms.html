Welcome to 1UPGaming — your premium PS5 rental service in Arunachal Pradesh! By booking a rental with us, you agree to the following terms and conditions. These rules exist to ensure a safe, fair, and enjoyable experience for everyone.

🏔️ 1. Eligibility Requirements

  • APST Only: This rental service is exclusively available for members of Arunachal Pradesh Scheduled Tribe (APST). Due to security reasons, we are unable to provide services to non-APST individuals at this time.
  • Age Requirement: All renters must be 18 years of age or older. If the primary user is a minor (under 18), a parent, guardian, or adult family member (18+) must be present, provide their details, and take full responsibility for the rental.
  • Valid ID Required: A valid government-issued ID — either Aadhaar Card or PAN Card — must be provided at the time of booking.
  • Geographic Restriction: Service is currently available only in Itanagar, Naharlagun, and Nirjuli in Arunachal Pradesh, India.
⚠️ Providing false information (including false APST claims or false age information) will result in immediate cancellation of the rental and potential legal action.

📋 2. Booking & Rental Process

  • Bookings are made through our website and confirmed via WhatsApp. A booking is only confirmed once you receive a confirmation reply from 1UPGaming on WhatsApp.
  • The customer selects their preferred game, rental plan (1 day / 3 days / 1 week), and provides delivery details through the booking form.
  • FC 26 (football game) is included free with every console rental. No additional charge for this game.
  • The rental period begins when the console is delivered to the customer and ends when it is collected/returned. Please be available for both delivery and collection.
  • Customers should specify their preferred delivery time slot when booking. Delivery is subject to availability.
  • Cancellations must be communicated via WhatsApp at least 2 hours before the scheduled delivery time.

⚠️ 3. Damage & Loss Policy

  • Customer Responsibility: The customer is fully responsible for the PS5 console, controllers, games, and all accessories from the time of delivery until collection.
  • Damage Charges: Any physical damage to the console, controllers, discs, or other accessories must be paid for in full by the customer. The cost will be assessed based on actual repair or replacement cost.
  • Loss: If the console, game, or any accessory is lost or stolen during the rental period, the customer is liable for the full replacement cost of the item.
  • Normal wear and tear (minor scratches from normal use) is expected and will not be charged.
  • Any pre-existing damage will be documented and photographed at the time of delivery, before it is handed over to the customer.
  • Important: Since there is NO security deposit, customers are trusted to return equipment in good condition. Misuse or damage will be recovered through legal means if necessary.
💡 Pro tip: Handle the PS5 with care — keep it in a stable, flat position, ensure good ventilation, and keep it away from liquids, dust, and young children.

🎮 4. Proper Use of Equipment

  • The PS5 console must not be misused, modified, or tampered with in any way, including attempts to jailbreak, mod, or install unauthorized software.
  • Do not loan or sublet the rented console to any third party. The console must remain with the person who made the booking at all times.
  • Do not attempt to repair or open the console yourself. If there is a technical issue, contact 1UPGaming immediately via WhatsApp.
  • The console must be used in a safe indoor environment. Do not use it outdoors or in extreme temperature/humidity conditions.
  • Only the game(s) provided with the rental should be used. Do not attempt to use pirated or unofficial game discs.
  • The console and accessories must be returned in the same condition as they were delivered — clean, undamaged, and with all original accessories.

  • Live Location Required: For delivery, the customer must share their live location on WhatsApp to ensure accurate and timely delivery.

  • At the time of collection (end of rental), the customer must return all items in person. If not possible, please coordinate with us in advance.
